The morning problem

Too much noise before the open

Most self-directed investors don’t need more random tips — they need a clearer starting point before the US session begins. This briefing organizes selected public information; it does not tell you what to trade.

Headlines arrive unsorted

Overnight news, index futures moves, and sector chatter hit at once — without a simple map of what you may want to review.

Research time disappears

Scrolling feeds eats the window you meant to use for calm, focused review before the bell.

Context gets skipped

Without sector and theme framing, it’s harder to decide which public headlines you may want to review later.

Delivery is scattered

Useful notes live across apps and tabs. A single daily brief keeps your briefing in one place on WhatsApp.
