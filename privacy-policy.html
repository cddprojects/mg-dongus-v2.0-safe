1. Information We Collect

When you complete the sign-up form on our website, we collect the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp phone number

We do not automatically collect browser data, cookies, IP addresses, or device identifiers through the sign-up form. If additional tracking tools are integrated in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To send you an invitation to the PreMarketGuide WhatsApp briefing group
  • To deliver the standard pre-market educational briefing via WhatsApp
  • To contact you with important updates related to the service

We do not use your information for paid advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating and communicating about PreMarketGuide.

3. WhatsApp and Third Parties

Our daily educational briefing is delivered through WhatsApp, a messaging platform operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. By joining the WhatsApp group, your phone number and any messages you send in the group are subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We encourage you to review WhatsApp's privacy policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not share your name, email address, or phone number with any other third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or affiliate partners.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as you remain a member of the PreMarketGuide WhatsApp group or until you request deletion.

If you leave the WhatsApp group or request that your data be deleted, we will remove your name, email address, and phone number from our records within 30 days.

5. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes. Your data is used exclusively to operate and deliver the PreMarketGuide briefing service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the personal information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

You are responsible for keeping your WhatsApp account secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal data at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: You may leave the WhatsApp group at any time with no obligation or penalty.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10.

8. Children's Privacy

PreMarketGuide is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted their information through our form, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of PreMarketGuide after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
